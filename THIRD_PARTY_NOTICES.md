## Amazon Fine Food Reviews

The private research benchmark refers to the dataset commonly known as
**Amazon Fine Food Reviews**:

- Kaggle dataset page:
  <https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/snap/amazon-fine-food-reviews>
- Stanford SNAP source page:
  <https://snap.stanford.edu/data/web-FineFoods.html>

The Kaggle page currently labels the dataset **CC0: Public Domain**. The
Stanford SNAP page describes 568,454 reviews from October 1999 through October
2012 and identifies fields including user IDs, profile names, product IDs,
ratings, summaries, and review text. That source page does not display a
separate dataset license.
